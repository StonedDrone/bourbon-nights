# BOURBON WALK — Game Loop Sketch

*Concept by JayNdaboX, 2026-09-18. "When I walk down Bourbon St I always try not to touch people."*

## Premise
Friday night on Bourbon Street. One unbroken run from Canal to Esplanade. You do not touch people. Ever.

## Core Loop (one block ≈ 60 seconds, ~13 blocks per run)
1. **Move** — weave through the crowd (tilt / stick / WASD).
2. **Read** — spot drifters, spill zones, and performer rings ahead.
3. **Dodge** — slip gaps, sidestep, hop curbs.
4. **Chain** — near-misses fill your Cool meter.

## Meters
- **Aura (health)** — 3 hits. Brush a shoulder = lose 1. Full bump = lose 2.
- **Cool (multiplier)** — near-misses raise it; any touch resets it to zero.
- **Buzz (difficulty)** — rises block by block as the night goes on: denser, drunker, louder crowd.
- **Cup (your drink)** — you start every run holding a full go-cup. Hard cuts, bumps, and slides slosh it; each hit spills some. An empty cup means no slow-mo sips. Finish a run with drink left for the **STEADY HANDS** bonus. GHOST + full cup = **SAINT** rank.

## Hazards
- **Drifters** — drunk tourists with a homing wobble, veer into you.
- **Performer rings** — street acts block the sidewalk, shrink your gaps.
- **Spill zones** — step in one and you slide forward uncontrolled.
- **Balcony bait** — people leaning out for beads, arms out = grab hitbox.
- **Second line** — every 3 blocks a parade crosses your route perpendicular, full street width. You can't go around it — time the gap between the band and the followers, or burn the umbrella.

- **Bead throws** — balconies hurl beads down with telegraphed landing zones, and catchers surge toward them in a scrambling cluster. Risk/reward: catch beads mid-air for the shield, get clipped and lose Aura.

## Pickups
- **Beads** — catch them mid-air for a shield: one free touch. (See also: bead throws. Same beads, different luck.)
- **Go-cup** — 3 seconds of slow-mo ("sip and weave").
- **Second-line umbrella** — clears a path through one crowd wave.

## Cups (pick your loadout before each run)
Two stats: **capacity** (how many slow-mo sips it holds) vs **stability** (how much it sloshes on hard cuts).
- **Go-cup** — the classic. Balanced. Unlocked from the start.
- **Styrofoam** — light and steady, barely sloshes on turns, but small: 2 sips.
- **Hurricane glass** — the tourist special. Huge capacity, sloshes if you breathe wrong. High risk, high reward.
- **Can** — tiny, nearly spill-proof, exactly 1 sip. For purists.
- **Fishbowl** — novelty monster. Endless sips, spills on every hard cut. Chaos pick.
Fancier cups unlock with GHOST and SAINT runs.

## Levels — seven nights, one boss
Levels are days of the week. Finish a night to unlock the next; ranks tracked per night.
- **Monday — Industry Night.** Thin crowd, slow. Tutorial night: learn the weave.
- **Tuesday — Taco Tuesday.** Crowd picks up. First drifters.
- **Wednesday — Hump Day.** Mid pace. Performer rings debut.
- **Thursday — Thirsty Thursday.** College crowd. Spill zones debut, Buzz climbs.
- **Friday — The Gauntlet.** The full run: everything on, parades every 3 blocks.
- **Saturday — Prime Time.** Densest regular crowd. Bead throws constant, parades every 2 blocks.
- **Sunday — Saints Day.** Slower, hungover — but unpredictable. Drunks and church crowd mix.
- **END BOSS: MARDI GRAS WEEKEND.** The street becomes one continuous parade — crossings back-to-back, bead storms nonstop, floats as moving walls, everything maxed. Three phases: Friday (the approach), Saturday (the wall), Sunday (the encore). SAINT rank on Mardi Gras weekend is the ultimate bragging right.

## Win / Lose
- Reach Esplanade = run complete, ranked by touches. **Zero touches = GHOST rank.**
- Lose all Aura = "YOU GOT HUGGED." Game over.

## Scoring
Distance + near-miss chains × Cool multiplier. Ghost runs unlock the "Untouchable" fit.
