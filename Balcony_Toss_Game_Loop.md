# BALCONY TOSS — Game Loop Sketch

*Companion game to Bourbon Walk. Same street, other side of the beads.*

## Premise
You're on a Bourbon Street balcony, bag of beads, crowd below holding their cups up. Land beads in cups. That's the whole religion.

## Core Loop
1. **Pick** — choose your target in the crowd below.
2. **Aim** — drag to set angle and power.
3. **Lead** — targets walk, so throw where they're going, not where they are.
4. **Watch** — arc physics, wind gusts, the drop.

## Targets (cup difficulty tiers — same cups as Bourbon Walk)
- **Fishbowl** — huge target, easy points. The tourist special.
- **Hurricane glass** — wide mouth, decent points.
- **Go-cup** — standard target, standard points.
- **Styrofoam** — small, good points.
- **Can** — tiny opening, massive points. The sniper shot.

## Scoring
- Landing = cup's point value × streak multiplier.
- Consecutive landings build the streak — **BEADSTORM** at 10 straight.
- Rim bounce that drops in anyway = **RATTLED IN**, double points.

## Hazards
- **Face hit** — miss the cup, hit the holder. Crowd anger rises; fill it and they start throwing beads back at YOU.
- **Wind gusts** — telegraphed, push your arc sideways.
- **Cup switchers** — some partiers swap cups mid-aim to bait you.

## Two ways to play
- **Saint** — lob beads gently into cups. Cheers, streaks, BEADSTORM.
- **Menace** — peg the cup or the hand holding it. The cup teeters — **FUMBLE!** — and if it drops, that's a **DROP!**: big chaos points, splash zone, and one very angry partier.
- Drops spike crowd anger twice as fast as face hits. High risk, high payout.

## Shared world
The beads raining down in Bourbon Walk's bead throws? Those are Balcony Toss players. And every drink dropped in Balcony Toss becomes a **spill zone** on the street in Bourbon Walk. Same street, same night, two games — your chaos is someone else's obstacle.
