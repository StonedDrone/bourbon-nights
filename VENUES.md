# Bourbon Nights — Venue Lineup

The real Bourbon Street spots that anchor the game's world, ordered from the
Canal Street end to the Esplanade Avenue end. Block ranges are approximate and
meant for game placement, not GPS navigation.

## How to Use These

- **Bourbon Walk:** each block between cross streets gets one venue's flavor —
  its crowd type, its hazard, its music.
- **Balcony Toss:** flagged balconies (Pat O'Brien's patio, Bourbon Pub,
  Mambo's) are the premium throw spots with bigger crowds and better rewards.
- Nights progress up the street: Monday starts quiet at Canal, Mardi Gras
  Weekend boss fight takes over the whole strip.
