# Bourbon Nights

Two Bourbon Street party games in one shared world. One street, two ways to cause chaos.

## The games

**Bourbon Walk** — Run the full length of Bourbon Street (Canal to Esplanade)
carrying a drink. Dodge the crowd, don't spill. Your chaos is someone else's
obstacle.

**Balcony Toss** — You're on the balcony. Toss beads into cups below — or knock
drinks loose. What you throw becomes a hazard in Bourbon Walk.

## The week (levels)

Monday — Industry Night (tutorial) · Tuesday — Taco Tuesday ·
Wednesday — Hump Day · Thursday — Thirsty Thursday · Friday — The Gauntlet ·
Saturday — Prime Time · Sunday — Saints Day · **Mardi Gras Weekend** (3-phase boss)
